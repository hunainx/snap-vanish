# Snap Vanish privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Snap Vanish does not collect, store or send any personal data.

- **Camera and microphone.** Video and audio are processed only inside your browser, on your computer, while you are on Google Meet, Microsoft Teams or Zoom in the browser. No frame, image or sound is uploaded, saved to disk or shared with anyone, including the developer.
- **Models.** All AI models and runtimes ship inside the extension. Nothing is downloaded at run time, and the extension makes no network requests of its own.
- **Settings.** Your settings (on/off, sensitivity, dust duration and direction, and the checkboxes) are kept with Chrome's `storage` API. If you use Chrome sync, Chrome syncs them across your own browsers.
- **Analytics.** None. No tracking, no cookies, no accounts.
- **Permissions.** Only `storage`. The extension runs only on meet.google.com, teams.microsoft.com, teams.live.com, teams.cloud.microsoft, zoom.us and zoom.com pages.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/hunainx/snap-vanish/issues
