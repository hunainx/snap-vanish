    Getting a clean vanish

    • Sit still for a few seconds after the camera starts, then lean left and right once. Every bit of wall the camera sees behind you is used as the real background; the rest is filled in by the on-device AI.
    • Snap with your hand clearly in view, palm roughly facing the camera, about chest height.
    • Snap again to come back. Your mic unmutes as you reappear.
    • If the camera moves, use Re-learn the room.
